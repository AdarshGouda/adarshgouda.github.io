Case study 03 · Fluid mechanics

Computational fluid dynamics

Two complementary investigations: implementing an interactive stable-fluids solver and translating turbulence-model choices into practical guidance for design engineers.

Navier–StokesRANSPython · PhiFlow
01 Problem02 Approach03 System04 Decisions05 Result06 Lessons

Problem

CFD produces persuasive images, but the engineering value comes from selecting the right abstraction and understanding what it cannot resolve. This work asked two related questions: how can a fluid solver remain stable enough for interaction, and how should a designer select among common RANS turbulence models?

Approach

For the solver study, I implemented the logic behind Jos Stam's stable-fluids method using Python and PhiFlow, tracing advection, diffusion, forces and pressure projection. For turbulence guidance, I compared the assumptions and practical behavior of k-ε, k-ω and SST families in common design situations.

System

The numerical loop makes each physical operation inspectable. The turbulence-model comparison adds the complementary, higher-level question of how modeled near-wall and free-stream behavior affects a design conclusion.

Engineering Decisions

Stability for interactivity

Use semi-Lagrangian advection and implicit steps to favor stable behavior at useful time steps.

Separate numerical operations

Keep forcing, transport and projection conceptually distinct so behavior can be explained and debugged.

Model follows the flow

Choose turbulence treatment from near-wall needs, adverse gradients, separation and free-stream sensitivity.

Evidence over aesthetics

Judge convergence, sensitivity and physical consistency before accepting a compelling contour plot.

Result

The implementation produced stable, interactive fluid behavior and a clearer understanding of how the Navier–Stokes terms become a computational sequence. The model-selection article turned that foundation into practical guidance for engineers using RANS tools in product design.

Lessons Learned

  • Numerical stability and physical accuracy are related but not interchangeable.
  • A turbulence model is a set of assumptions about unresolved physics, not a menu preference.
  • Mesh, boundary conditions and near-wall treatment can matter more than solver branding.
  • Good simulation practice makes the chain from assumption to design decision auditable.

About / point of view

I enjoy the creative process of bringing ideas to life.

Especially when the answer is not obvious, the disciplines overlap and engineering judgment matters.

What I do

I connect the machine, the model and the mission.

My foundation is mechanical engineering, but my work has steadily expanded through electromechanical design, controls, embedded systems, robotics and machine learning.

I am most useful on complex technology where no single discipline owns the answer. I can move from first principles and numerical models to architecture, build, test and product decisions, while keeping a cross-functional team aligned around evidence.

Leadership, to me, is not distance from the technical work. It is the ability to frame the right problem, create clarity at the interfaces and help specialists make the whole system succeed.

Operating principles

How I approach engineering.

Start with physics

Make constraints and failure modes visible before choosing an architecture.

Model to decide

A model should reduce a real uncertainty, not exist because simulation is available.

Integrate early

Interfaces hide risk. Put the system together soon enough to learn while choices remain reversible.

Earn confidence

Trace conclusions to assumptions, tests and repeatable evidence.

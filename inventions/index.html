Inventive practice

Find leverage at the interfaces.

Many useful inventions begin where established subsystems meet: a measurement that is difficult to obtain, an architecture that removes a constraint, or a passive behavior that simplifies control.

My approach is to define the physical contradiction clearly, search the prior art, separate the inventive concept from one implementation and then test whether the claim still has engineering value when the details change.
